Where we actually are

  • Exists: the show is registered and commissionable — bible (two hosts, no supervision, "may not be strictly authorised"), 12 traits, commercial format written in (fake sponsor, real-time undermining, too-fast disclaimer).
  • Exists: the pipeline — brief in, mastered episode out ~20–35 min later, aired like a song. June Ferry's show runs on it daily.
  • Missing: voices. Duke sits on carlin (= Bob), Nyx on lani (= old Lani). Two characters sharing a voice with existing hosts breaks the fiction — this is the blocker.
  • Missing: a schedule, event triggers, commercial policy, and your taste on every dial below.
  • Episodes so far: zero. This form is the green light spec.
